How this works

Labels start as a detailed identification pass by vision agents (each studied one zoomed section), with your own corrections layered on top. The mark after a coordinate says where a label came from: ? the agent was unsure — check these first; ≈ carried over from a colour sibling you corrected; ✓ you named it. Everything autosaves in your browser; hit Export when done.

Gold chips under a card are what the shipped game currently draws with that tile — CLASS:, monster:, item(ring):, BOSS:, shop:, terrain: — read straight out of the content tables in src/. A card with a gold dot is in use; a card without one is art the game has never drawn. Use only in-game to audit what is mapped and only unused to find something better to map it to. If a chip is wrong, the label you type is the correction — the tile is what it is, and the table follows the tile.

A few tiles sit in two overlapping subsheets and so appear on two cards (the editor tells you when). Editing either one now updates both, so a stale twin can't overwrite your work on export.

On a phone: tap any sprite to open the editor. Tap a category to stamp it (that also marks it reviewed), or ✓ Looks right to accept the guess as-is and move on. Prev/Next walk the sheet in order; Skip to next unreviewed jumps ahead. Tools (top right) opens bulk select/name, the sheet jump menu, and Import/Reset.

On a desktop: click a sprite to select it (the gold anchor); ←↑↓→ move · Shift+click or Shift+arrows range-select · press a category hotkey to stamp the selection · Enter edit the name, Enter again jumps to the next · N next unreviewed. Use Select row/col/sheet + a category chip or the Name selection box ({i} = auto-number) for whole families at once.
