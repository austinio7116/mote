The weapon block, read as a set

Source columns 16–24, rows 29–33 are one melee set: nine weapon types across, five materials down. Columns 25–30 are the ranged set on the same rows. The game’s seventeen weapons are scattered through it and most landed on the wrong type, because three different subsheets cut the block up and nobody read it as a block. This mapping is now live in the game.

What I think each column is

Broad blade
Wide, flared, gem at the base. Short for its width -- a falchion or a great sword rather than a long one.
Dagger
The same blade, small.
Long sword
Long, straight, thin. The clearest column in the block.
Pick-axe
Angled head on a brown haft.
Spear
Thin shaft, small leaf point.
Trident
Three prongs -- two outer tines and a centre spike. Read as a mace on the first pass, which is what put the trident on a gold bow.
Battle axe
Shaft with a big curved bit, backed in navy.
Mace head
A spiked ball on a short haft. The haft settles it: a mace, not a shuriken. There is no shuriken anywhere in the block.
War hammer
Chunky rectangular head.
Bow
Curve and string.
Crossbow
The three crossbow-ish columns are the ones I am least sure about.
Staff / wand
Gem at the tip. The wands already use this column.
Arrow / bolt
A bare shaft.

Where the seventeen now sit

The materials do the work: they give each weapon type a power tier without needing new art, so the mace and the morning star are one head in steel and gold, and Frost Brand and Flame Tongue are the long sword in ice and fire.
