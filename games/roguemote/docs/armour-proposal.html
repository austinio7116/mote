The armour block, as I read it

Source columns 16–24, rows 21–26 are one set: seven pieces across, five colours down, with two empty gutter columns. The game’s thirteen armour items are scattered through it and most of them landed on furniture and bones, because nobody had read the block as a block. Everything below is a proposal — nothing is applied.

What I think each column is

Open helm
A helm seen face-on with a vertical nose guard.
Great helm
Full helm with a banded brow and a visor slit. Row 22 is a low brimmed cap instead.
Wizard hat
Your call: this whole column is wizard hats. The horned helm was sitting on the brown one.
Hood
Rounded, two dark eye holes. You called (20,23) a hood.
Cuirass
Flared shoulders over a panelled chest plate. Row 21 is a soft tunic with buttons.
Hauberk
A collared body piece with an open neck and two studs.
Round shield
Face-on, with a centre boss.
Columns 19 and 20 -- the wizard hats and hoods, five colours of each -- still carry no item. Ten unused pieces of head armour.
